He walks with a quiet kind of fire,
Not chasing crowns, but climbing higher.
From lines of code to dreams unseen,
He builds tomorrow on a glowing screen.

Some days are Node, some days AI,
Some bugs refuse to say goodbye.
Yet every error, every fall,
Becomes another lesson after all.

He doesn't need the biggest name,
Or instant fortune, wealth, or fame.
He wants the chance to prove one thing—
That patient hands can build anything.

From Durgapur's familiar ground,
He searches for where paths are found.
A fresher today, perhaps—but still,
With every project, stronger will.

He learns, he breaks, he starts anew,
Turns scattered thoughts to something true.
And while the world may not yet see
The developer he's meant to be—

He's writing more than code each day,
He's shaping who he'll be along the way.
No perfect road, no guaranteed start,
Just stubborn hope and a builder's heart.

And someday, when he looks behind,
He'll see the nights he chose to grind—
And quietly smile, because he'll know:
The person he became was built one line at a time.
